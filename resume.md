## Projects

### Pell Grant Graduation Outcomes at HBCUs and Other Institutions
Analyzed 2022–23 IPEDS data for 5,736 U.S. institutions to study how graduation rates of Pell Grant recipients differ by sector, level, and HBCU status. Used multiple linear regression in Python and found that a 14.7-point HBCU gap shrinks to near zero once the share of Pell students is accounted for.

### Same Grant, Different Outcomes: Interactive Data Story
Designed a seven-slide data story in Flourish that moves from the sector gap to the HBCU gap, using color to guide attention to the key comparison. [View the live story](https://public.flourish.studio/story/3869663/)

### Equity Research on Black Male Enrollment, Howard University
Conducted equity research on Black male enrollment at Howard University and peer institutions to identify disparities and assess institutional performance benchmarks. Presented findings at the American Educational Research Association (AERA) Annual Meeting 2026.
